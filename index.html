About the Author

Mr. Davis Atuahene Agyei

Mr. Davis Atuahene Agyei is a Ghanaian pastor, teacher, lecturer, author, and thought leader. He serves at GM Cathedral and is the Executive Director and Lead Consultant of the Atlantic Institute of Development and Education (AIDE).

Through his books, he explores important biblical teachings on faith, redemption, temptation, the Church, and God's purpose for humanity and the nations.

Experienced Educator

Years of teaching and mentoring students and young people.

Published Author

A collection of books on faith, education, and spiritual growth.

Community Advocate

Committed to creating positive change through education and God's Word.
